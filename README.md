## What you need

- A free **Google account** - we work in [Google Colab](https://colab.research.google.com), which runs Python in your browser. There is **nothing to install**.
- A browser (Chrome or Firefox work best) and Zoom.
- No prior programming experience. Really.

## How to use these materials

1. Browse to a notebook (for example `Day 1 → Sessions → Day1_Session1_Python_Basics.ipynb`).
2. Click the **"Open in Colab"** badge at the top of the notebook - it opens ready to run.
3. In Colab, immediately click **File → Save a copy in Drive** so your own work is saved.

Full setup instructions (with screenshots of every step described) are at the top of the first notebook - we will also walk through them together at the start of Day 1.

## Where the data comes from

- **Gapminder** (life expectancy, population, GDP for 142 countries, 1952-2007): the [Gapminder Foundation](https://www.gapminder.org/data/), whose data is free to use under a CC-BY license. Our file is the classic teaching extract distributed in [plotly's datasets](https://github.com/plotly/datasets). Hundreds more indicators are available on their site.
- **German federal election turnout**: official figures from the Federal Returning Officer ([bundeswahlleiterin.de](https://www.bundeswahlleiterin.de)).
- **German city populations**: compiled for this course; official statistics at [Destatis](https://www.destatis.de), Germany's Federal Statistical Office.
- The small helper files (country facts, Nordic countries) were written for this course.
